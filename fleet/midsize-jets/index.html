THE AIRCRAFT GUIDE

Midsize jets.
Your possibilities.

More room to settle in, with cabin layouts suited to working or relaxing. A versatile category when you want additional comfort without the largest cabin.

REPRESENTATIVE SEATING6–8 passengers
CONSIDER FORRegional & longer domestic trips
AVAILABILITYConfirmed per enquiry

A cabin to consider

A midsize cabin can suit a traveling management team or a family wanting additional personal space. Compare cabin layout and baggage access rather than choosing by category name alone.

What to confirm

Nonstop capability depends on the particular aircraft, passengers, baggage and conditions. A fuel stop may still be necessary on a longer itinerary.

Aircraft families

Aircraft families to discuss include the Citation XLS series and Hawker 800/900 series. Names identify examples only; this website does not list available aircraft or imply any operator relationship.
