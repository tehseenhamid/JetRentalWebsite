ARRIVE TOGETHER

One shared itinerary. More time together.

From a leadership retreat to an event weekend, group charter starts with the details: the size of your party, the luggage and the schedule everyone needs to keep.

Match the group to the cabin

Seating capacity alone is not enough. Bulky equipment, golf bags and event materials can change the aircraft requirement. Larger groups may need an executive airliner or multiple aircraft.

Plan around the event

Busy events can put pressure on aircraft, airport access and ground transport. Request options early and establish a fallback arrival window before committing to a tightly timed schedule.

Keep traveler coordination simple

Assign a single organizer for passenger information, itinerary updates and dietary requests. Supply sensitive identity details only through the operator’s approved secure process.

A LITTLE CLARITY

A few things you may be wondering.

All questions, answered↗
How much does a private jet charter cost?+

A charter price depends on the aircraft, route, dates, positioning and operating conditions. Ask for an itemized written proposal including taxes, airport charges and any additional services. The planning worksheet on this site illustrates arithmetic; it is not a live quote.

Can I arrange a one-way flight?+

Yes, one-way itineraries are a common charter request. Pricing can include aircraft positioning, so a one-way fare is not necessarily half the cost of a return itinerary.

Do I need a membership to charter a jet?+

On-demand charter is normally booked trip by trip. A jet card, membership or fractional program is a separate arrangement with its own financial commitment, access rules and contract.
