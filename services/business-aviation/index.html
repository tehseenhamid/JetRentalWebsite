MAKE ROOM FOR WHAT MATTERS

Your business moves. Your travel should follow.

Build an itinerary around meetings, site visits and the people who need to be there. A thoughtful charter brief helps executive assistants and travel teams compare practical options.

One brief for the entire itinerary

For multi-city travel, include every stop, meeting location and realistic ground-transfer buffer. Share any hard arrival deadlines so the proposal can distinguish essential timing from preferences.

A cabin that supports the day

Discuss seating arrangements, work surfaces, privacy and connectivity. Wi-Fi coverage and performance vary by aircraft and route; request confirmation if a live call or presentation is essential.

A proposal your team can approve

Ask for itemized pricing, payment deadlines, traveler-change rules and the point of contact for itinerary changes. For repeated travel, compare charter costs with the terms of a card or other access program.

A LITTLE CLARITY

A few things you may be wondering.

All questions, answered↗
How much does a private jet charter cost?+

A charter price depends on the aircraft, route, dates, positioning and operating conditions. Ask for an itemized written proposal including taxes, airport charges and any additional services. The planning worksheet on this site illustrates arithmetic; it is not a live quote.

Can I arrange a one-way flight?+

Yes, one-way itineraries are a common charter request. Pricing can include aircraft positioning, so a one-way fare is not necessarily half the cost of a return itinerary.

Do I need a membership to charter a jet?+

On-demand charter is normally booked trip by trip. A jet card, membership or fractional program is a separate arrangement with its own financial commitment, access rules and contract.
