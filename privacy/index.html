WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

Last reviewed September 22, 2026.

Scope

This demonstration website does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or third-party fonts. Core content and images are served from the same site.

Enquiry information

The form collects itinerary details, name, email and optional phone, company and notes. On this static demonstration, form details remain in the current page and are not submitted or saved. No aviation company receives them. Please use fictional test details.

How information is used

The static demonstration uses entered information only to illustrate the enquiry experience. The form does not transmit data for sales or marketing. The browser form holds information only for the current page session; no lead data is stored in localStorage.

Retention and deletion

This static preview does not create enquiry records. Use “Plan another journey” to clear the form. Your browser may separately offer autofill. GitHub Pages may log visitor IP addresses as part of its hosting service.

Before a real business launch

A production operator must replace this notice with its own accurate controller identity, contact details, purposes, legal basis where applicable, processors, retention schedule, rights process and international-transfer information. Accepting real enquiries requires a separately configured backend or form service. This demonstration notice is not a substitute for business-specific review.
