A LITTLE CLARITY

A few things you may be wondering.

All questions, answered↗
How much does a private jet charter cost?+

A charter price depends on the aircraft, route, dates, positioning and operating conditions. Ask for an itemized written proposal including taxes, airport charges and any additional services. The planning worksheet on this site illustrates arithmetic; it is not a live quote.

Can I arrange a one-way flight?+

Yes, one-way itineraries are a common charter request. Pricing can include aircraft positioning, so a one-way fare is not necessarily half the cost of a return itinerary.

Do I need a membership to charter a jet?+

On-demand charter is normally booked trip by trip. A jet card, membership or fractional program is a separate arrangement with its own financial commitment, access rules and contract.
