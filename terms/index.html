WEBSITE INFORMATION

Website terms.

Last reviewed September 22, 2026.

Demonstration status

Velora Air is a fictional website concept. Browsing, calculating an example or submitting a test enquiry does not create a charter contract, reserve an aircraft or arrange air transportation.

Educational material

Aircraft categories, seating ranges, route discussions and the cost worksheet are illustrative planning information. They are not certified specifications, real-time operating advice, availability statements or binding prices.

Images and names

Images are AI-generated illustrations of generic aircraft and cabins. Aircraft family names are used for identification and education only. No endorsement or relationship with a manufacturer, operator or competitor is implied.

External information

External references are provided to help visitors locate primary information. Airport, regulatory and commercial details can change and must be verified for the individual journey.

Live-business replacement

Before launch, the owner must publish its actual identity and role, applicable disclosures and reviewed website terms. A separate charter agreement should state operating-carrier information, payment terms, cancellation rules and responsibilities.
