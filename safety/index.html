CONFIDENCE, CONSIDERED

Ask the questions
that matter.

A beautiful cabin is only one detail. Before committing, understand who operates the flight and how the trip has been assessed.

Know the operator

Ask for the legal name of the direct air carrier and confirmation of its authority to perform the proposed flight. A broker and an operator play different roles; the company taking an enquiry may not be the carrier.

Confirm aircraft and crew suitability

Request the proposed aircraft details, seating and baggage limits. Ask the provider about its process for reviewing crew qualifications, aircraft status, operating conditions and any substitutions.

Understand independent assessments

If a business refers to ARGUS, WYVERN or another assessment, ask what the rating covers and whether it is current for the relevant entity. A logo alone does not establish the status of your specific flight.

Read the operational and commercial plan

Ask about insurance, cancellations, weather disruptions, replacement aircraft and additional costs. The contract should make responsibilities and options clear before payment.

Start with primary guidance

The FAA Safe Air Charter guidance explains how travelers can identify legitimate charter operations. Consult the carrier and the appropriate authority for current trip-specific requirements.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

A useful checklist.

  • Identify the operating carrier
  • Verify the proposed aircraft
  • Understand the review process
  • Confirm contract terms and insurance
  • Agree on the disruption plan
