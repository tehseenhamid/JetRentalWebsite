THE VELORA JOURNAL

The opportunity behind an empty leg.

Understand private jet empty legs, flexible itinerary requirements, cancellation risks and the questions to ask before accepting a repositioning flight.

Velora Air editorial concept · Reviewed September 22, 2026

Why an empty leg exists

An aircraft may need to reposition for another passenger flight or return to a base. A provider can sometimes offer that sector to a traveler whose route and schedule align with the movement.

Why flexibility matters

The underlying booking determines the opportunity. Its passenger may change plans, the aircraft may change, or operational conditions may shift the flight. Treat the listing as provisional until the carrier confirms the arrangement.

Ask what happens if the flight changes

Read the cancellation and refund terms. Clarify whether a replacement flight is included, separately priced or unavailable. A lower fare does not establish a recovery guarantee.

Consider the complete journey

An empty leg usually addresses one sector. Think about the return journey, transfers and any extra positioning. A deal that creates an expensive or inconvenient onward trip may not meet your original goal.

Use verified availability

Look for a clear source and confirmation process rather than an unverified countdown or static fare. This website intentionally lists no live empty legs because it has no connected operator inventory.
